# Methods and limitations

## Corpus
- 849 initial bibliographic entries across Scopus and Web of Science exports.
- 571 deduplicated records, of which 568 remained active after the documented record correction.
- The agreed final analytical corpus has 350 records. This public package presents these 350 IDs and their bibliographic metadata, not every intermediate screening decision.

## Figures
- Annual production and document types use all 350 included records.
- Journal rankings use journal articles and reviews only (n = 208).
- Country rankings use author affiliations where country is available (n = 266) and full counting; a multi-country publication can count for more than one country.
- Keyword frequencies are derived from records with usable keywords (n = 217); publication counts can overlap across keywords.
- The first-authorship figure uses 341 individual-author publications (274 women and 67 men); nine publications have institutional authorship.
- Of 341 first authors classified, the research team reports 290 automated-assisted public-profile checks and 51 manually examined cases. Individual profile evidence for all 290 automatic-assisted checks is not included here, and gender is not a direct metadata field in the source databases. Assess uncertainty and reproducibility accordingly.

## Limits
- No raw full texts or substantial third-party abstract text are redistributed.
- The current release does not include an executable pipeline reproducing each cleaned bibliometric field from the proprietary Scopus/WoS exports.
- Exploratory automatic classifications of violence manifestation and methods are not included as final outcomes.
- Bibliometric counts describe the literature, **not** the population prevalence of violence.
- Analyses of professional groups, violence types or platform usage need reconciled human validation before a final coded dataset is released.
